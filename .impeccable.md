# Design Context

Confirmed with Andrew on November 12, 2026. This supersedes the historical
TGHE / private-equity positioning in REDO-IDEATION.md, which is preserved.

## Audience and purpose

CTOs, engineering leaders, and people who can introduce Andrew to those leaders.
Visitors may arrive through a personal conversation, a forwarded presentation,
a training, a practical guide, or his public engineering work. Help them understand
where his judgment is useful and how to start working with him.

Ongoing technical advisory is the main offer. Focused reviews, working sessions,
and tailored training are possible starting engagements. Public material should
be useful on its own and lead naturally to a conversation about applying it.
Do not fabricate client testimonials, downloadable trainings, pricing, or case studies.

## Personality

Practical, curious, candid. A systems builder working beside an experienced leader.
Use Andrew's voice guide. Explain what he brings and how the engagement works;
avoid a resume, a consulting manifesto, inflated promises, and sales funnel language.

## Visual direction

Andrew explicitly selected his blog and Plainsight as the family references:
navy #0e1524 framing, ivory #fafaf5 reading surfaces, brass #d4ad5a accents,
self-hosted Plex Serif and Plex Mono, textured night-workshop illustration.
Those explicit font choices override generic skill font exclusions.
Thin rules, generous reading space, square edges, clear typographic hierarchy.
No generic service-card grid, gradients, invented metrics, or decorative dashboards.
The shared worktable illustration gives advisory its own identity within the family.

## Implementation

Keep Hugo and the existing Cloudflare Worker identity. The public host is
advisory.andrewphunter.com, activated November 12, 2026. Andrew explicitly chose
to drop consulting.andrewphunter.com without a redirect; its domain binding and
DNS record were removed. Do not restore the old hostname.
Andrew retired the original consulting articles and the insights archive on
November 12, 2026: their positioning is off brand for this advisory practice.
Do not restore them or link to them. The homepage's experience section should
describe AI, transformation, leadership and delivery, and public work through
concepts and practical experience, rather than a list of named employers.
About should explain what Andrew brings and how he has personally applied it.
Use industry and operating context (for example, a large wireless carrier or a
fintech) for selected examples. Organize around his approach and judgment, not
career chronology. LinkedIn supplies the named-employer professional record;
Plainsight remains named as his public lab.
The Resources page ends with one accent band for team training and working
sessions, including its contact link. Do not append the generic contact band
below it; that repeats the action and stacks two grey surfaces before the footer.
Support small screens and keyboard navigation.

Resource topic pages collect external reading and published local materials. The
main Resources page remains a short selection (at most three items per topic).
Trainings, decks, and guides use stable page-bundle landing URLs, with optional
materials and related reading. Keep future content in drafts; do not fill the
public library with placeholder courses or empty format categories.

Topic collections paginate at 12 items, with ordinary numbered links. Search
spans the full resource library and offers topic and format filters. Keep search
state shareable in the URL. Show only formats with available material. Use the
existing editorial rows and native form controls rather than adding a card grid.
