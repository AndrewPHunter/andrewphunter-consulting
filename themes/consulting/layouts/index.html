More about me →

AI

I've built AI platforms and internal agents that teams use in everyday work, including mapping system dependencies and preparing code changes for review. Getting a return means choosing useful problems and accounting for the integration, evaluation, and operating costs. I bring the engineering and business judgment to work through both.

Transformation

My career has included transformations at progressively larger scales, from a client platform to an engineering organization to an enterprise. That work connects business priorities, system architecture, and the sequence of changes teams can carry out while the business keeps running. I've worked through those decisions with executives and implemented the changes alongside engineers.

Leadership & delivery

I've led engineering teams, developed managers and technical leads, and restored delivery reliability. That experience also shapes how I use coding agents: clear responsibilities, bounded work, and review that checks what was actually built. The aim is to help engineers manage more work while retaining the judgment and accountability needed to ship it.

Public work

I build inference engines, model investigation tools, and GPU performance experiments to work through questions I want to understand. I publish the code, measurements, and reasoning, including approaches that didn't hold up. You can inspect the work and follow how I arrived at a conclusion.
